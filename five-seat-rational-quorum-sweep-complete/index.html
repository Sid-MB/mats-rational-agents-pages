Lower the bar for agreement and the table closes — on somebody's head

Five copies of the project's Bayesian-rational negotiator, private score sheets, private thresholds, one package out of 256. Every published number about this table was measured under unanimity, which is also the rule that hands every seat a veto — so "five rational seats rarely agree" and "any one of five rational seats can block" were inseparable. This sweep separates them: same frozen bank, same 120-episode grid per cell, varying only how many accepting seats a deal needs (5, 4 or 3 of 5) crossed with two deadlines (4 and 16 rounds). The deadline axis is the control that kills the clock explanation from inside the same experiment.

Relaxing the rule rescues closure completely, and the clock does nothing. Deal rate runs 0.217 [0.125, 0.317] → 0.492 [0.358, 0.625] → 0.908 [0.808, 0.983] as the quorum falls from 5 to 4 to 3, while unanimity closes 0.217 [0.125, 0.317] at four rounds and 0.217 [0.125, 0.317] at sixteen. The price is the programme's cleanest guarantee: individual-rationality violations per episode go 0.000 [0.000, 0.000] → 0.242 [0.142, 0.358] → 1.108 [0.858, 1.342], Gini rises +0.344 [+0.190, +0.479] against unanimity, and the winning coalition shrinks to exactly the quorum. The extra deals are not the deal the blocked table was failing to find; they are minimum-winning-coalition deals that leave an excluded seat below its own walk-away point about two thirds of the time.

The two-factor extension: pricing the veto on its own

These two cells additionally dissolve it, moving two protocol features at once. They are reported separately and are never pooled into the grid above.

Who pays for the extra deals

A quorum close is a deal that passed without every seat's support; the outvoted seat is one absent from the closing offer's support set. The zeros in the unanimity rows are a self-test of the detector rather than a finding — no outvoted seat can exist when closure requires everybody — so a non-zero majority number cannot be a detector artifact.
