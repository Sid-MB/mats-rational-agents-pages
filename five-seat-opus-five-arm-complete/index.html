Headline comparison

Five rational minus one rational: −0.4976 normalized score, 95% CI [−0.5901, −0.4036], and −53.3 percentage points of deal rate, 95% CI [−62.5, −43.3]. One rational agent was much less brittle than an entirely rational table, but five Opus agents still performed best.
