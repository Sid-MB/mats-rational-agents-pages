Does wanting the table's welfare change the table?

The five-arm campaign replaced frontier LLM negotiators with agents that are better at getting their own way. This extension seats agents that are exactly as capable and want something else: the same composed belief / optimal-stopping / best-response negotiator with its objective column swapped from own surplus to the table's normalized Nash welfare. Same 24 frozen games, same seeds, same private information, same Opus seats, paired episode by episode.

Both co-primaries, side by side

Each family of agents optimizes a different quantity, and each leads on the one it optimizes — so neither column is reported alone. utilitarian score is what the self-interested agents maximize; normalized Nash welfare is the fairness objective's own coordinate. Deal rate and the distributional columns are the referees. Intervals resample the 24 parameter sets. Extension arms are marked with a rule on the left; all_selfish_dp_oracle is a control, not a result — it is the same full-information composed DP with the own-surplus objective, which is what makes the fairness contrast one-variable rather than a comparison across two different decision surfaces.
